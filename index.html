How to use this map

  • Click a circle to see a summary of that funder, PAC, candidate or measure.
  • In the summary, click the name to open its full info sheet. Names in the sheet link to other sheets.
  • The bigger the bubble or thicker the line, the more money flowing to it.
  • Hover over or tap a line to see the amount.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and use reset view to start over.
